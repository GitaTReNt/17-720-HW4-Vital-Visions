Part 1: Smartphone-based heart-rate monitoring
Record a 30-second finger-over-camera video with torch on. Per frame: grayscale conversion,
mean intensity, DC subtraction, high-pass filter, FFT maximum in 60-100 bpm range,
peak finding with minimum distance based on estimated HR and camera sampling rate.
Deliver FFT plot with HR in Hz; time-domain plot with detected beats and bpm title; code.
Optional: no flash and different finger pressures.

Part 2: Fine-tuning vision models for medical image diagnostics
Choose a pretrained model and medical image dataset; choices beyond listed datasets allowed.
Fine-tune and report validation accuracy. Submit a Colab notebook and brief writeup
with model, data and results. Optional per-class ROC/AUC and last-layer t-SNE.

Part 3: Real-time screen-camera communication with alpha channel
Static picture on transmitter screen; modulate alpha with BFSK for phone receiver.
Increase data rate and record visually noticeable changes. Test delta alpha 0.1 and 0.5,
at distances 20,40,80,120 cm. Report data rates and BER versus alpha at each distance,
and explain. BER=1-correct bits/transmitted bits. Data-rate=correct bits per second.
Optional multi-word demo recorded with a third camera.
